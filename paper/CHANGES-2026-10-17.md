## Three things disclosed rather than fixed

These need retraining or re-sampling and are left as author decisions. Suggested wording
is in `errata-2026-10-17.md` in the code repository.

1. **Improved denoising trains its reverse variance about 1000x too weakly** — the sampled
   variational term is weighted $10^{-3}$ without rescaling by the number of timesteps,
   where the reference weighting is equivalent to unity at $T=1000$. The baseline still
   reaches $6.04$ against the bridge's $9.83$ at fifty evaluations, so the deviation does
   not flatter the comparison.
2. **Variance restoration does not fire on the final bridge step** — `eta` is forced to
   zero there regardless of the variance profile, so the reported gains belong to that
   variant.
3. **DDIM retains the raw epsilon after clipping** where the reference rederives it from
   the clipped $x_0$. This bears on the *explanation* of high-step-count DDIM degradation,
   not on any number.

# Addendum, 18 October: text burnt into two figures

Reported by the team: Figure 2 in A.3 had "weird AI text" in the image. Confirmed and
fixed, together with a second figure carrying the same problem.

**11. `figures/gamma_sweep.pdf` (Figure 2, A.3) — regenerated.** The image carried a
nine-line prose footer of internal working notes, including the sentence *"The manuscript
tunes it without saying so"*, plus ASCII mathematics (`gamma^2 = sigma^2 T`,
`grad log p_s`) and an editorialising heading. None of it was caption material and some of
it was an internal critique of our own manuscript. `bridges/gamma_figure.py` was the only
figure generator without a `--bare` flag; it now has one and the figure is regenerated
with it. The panel titles drop the "(correct sampler)" / "(biased sampler)" judgements and
the legend drops the word "(published)" — the heavier line marks
$\boldsymbol{\gamma}=0.3162$ and the caption names it.

**Also corrected while there: that caption claimed "measured floor $0.70$".** The figure
plots `fid_train` at $n=10\,000$ on MNIST, whose measured holdout floor is $0.98$
(`runs/fid_floors.json`). Caption and generator default both updated. Same class of error
as edit 5.

**12. `figures/conditional_variance.pdf` (Figure 3, A.4) — regenerated.** Its prose footer
was already suppressed, but three things were not: a panel title hard-coding
*"Prop. 2.13"* (which would go stale against the manuscript's numbering, and the caption
already uses a proper `\ref`), a second panel title asserting *"The same two networks have
identical loss at every s, and differ only here"*, and a legend entry reading *"time input
on $[0,0.1)$ **as published**"*. All three are neutral under `--bare` now, and the caption
gained a clause identifying the two lower-panel curves, which the legend previously
carried.

## Why this was not caught earlier

Matplotlib renders this text as vector paths, not as text objects, so `pdftotext` returns
**zero characters** for both figures. No text-level scan of the sources or the built PDF
could have found it. The only reliable check is to rasterise each figure and look at it.
Worth doing once over any figure before submission.

Standing convention from here: **keep text in figures to the minimum that makes the axes
readable — titles, axis labels, legend, units. Everything explanatory goes in the LaTeX
caption.** Four of the seven generators already had `--bare` for this; all of them do now.
